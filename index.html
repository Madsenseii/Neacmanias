GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS?

Where and when is it?

5 and 6 November 2026 (Thursday and Friday) in Kolkata. Gates open at 10:00 on both days. The exact venue and entry details are announced on NEAC channels.

Does the Entry Pass include events?

No. The Entry Pass is for entry only. Competitions, workshops and cosplay are booked separately, or pick a combo to save.

How will I get my ticket?

Right after payment your personal ticket appears on screen with a unique ID and QR code. It is saved on this device under “My Tickets”, and you can download it as an image or PDF. Show the QR at the gate.

Can someone copy my ticket?

Every ticket carries a unique ID tied to your Razorpay Payment ID, and gate staff check it against the payment record. Each ticket is logged on entry, so a copy will be flagged as already used. Please do not share your ticket image publicly.

How do esports registrations work?

Esports tournaments are registered per squad (BGMI and Free Fire 4+1, Valorant 5+1, Minecraft per person). Slots are limited. Game titles, rules and prize details are shared at registration and may change.

I paid but did not get my ticket

Do not pay again. Open Find my ticket and enter your Payment ID (from the Razorpay SMS or email), the phone number you used and the amount you paid. Your ticket is created on the spot. If it still fails, email teamneac@gmail.com with the payment ID.

What about refunds?

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy. For help, write to teamneac@gmail.com.

Are guests and performers confirmed?

Performers and special guests will be revealed closer to the date. We only announce a name once it is confirmed in writing. Programme, timings and details are planning drafts subject to final confirmation of venue, guests and approvals.
